## Faster local transcription

MUTTER's dictation engine has been upgraded under the hood. Transcriptions
should be noticeably quicker for most people, and streaming models are now
supported — you can watch the text appear as you speak instead of waiting
for the recording to finish.

## Streaming overlay

A live overlay now shows the transcription in progress when you use a
streaming-capable model. If you'd rather not see it, switch to the minimal
overlay style in Advanced Settings.

## More model choices

The Models page now offers a wider range of local speech-to-text models to
pick from, spanning several model families and languages.

## Notes

This is a substantial upgrade to the transcription pipeline. Everything
should work out of the box, but if dictation behaves unexpectedly after
updating, try re-selecting your model on the Models page.
